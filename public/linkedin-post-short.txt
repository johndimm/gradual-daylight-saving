The DST debate has 3 options. There should be 4.

1. Keep the current system
2. Permanent standard time
3. Permanent daylight saving
4. Gradual daylight saving

What if your clock shifted by 32 seconds a day instead of 1 hour overnight?

Same sunlight benefits. No lost sleep. No jet lag. No legislative deadlock. Just a lookup table of 365 offsets that your phone applies automatically.

The entire system fits on an index card.
